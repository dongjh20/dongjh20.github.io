I am a Research Fellow in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the National University of Singapore (NUS).

I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Vehicle and Mobility, Tsinghua University, in 2025, under the supervision of [Prof. Keqiang Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=x58fnLQAAAAJ&hl=en). Before that, I received my B.E. degree from the School of Vehicle and Mobility, Tsinghua University, in 2020, with a minor degree in Computer Application.
